### Fixed
- On Linux, `clean` moves an item to the Trash through descriptors, as `--permanent` deletes one (since v0.2.0 the check and the move were by path). The item's folder is reached from `/` one folder at a time, never through a symbolic link, and the item is moved out of that folder only if its device and inode are still the plan's (`path_changed` otherwise): a folder on the way swapped for a symbolic link can no longer send an entry outside the plan to the Trash. The move never replaces an entry already in the Trash, whose `files` and `info` folders are never reached through a symbolic link either, and an entry that took the item's name just before the move is moved back where it was. Where the filesystem cannot rename without replacing (NFS; NTFS through ntfs-3g and other FUSE filesystems without it), the Trash is checked for the name just before the move, as `mv -n` does.
